Business Context

Ride-sharing runs on data

Every booking creates data across customers, drivers, locations, ride status, time, and revenue. Business teams need one connected view to make fast decisions.

DemandIdentify peak hours and high-demand zones
RevenueTrack earnings, fares, and high-value segments
OperationsMonitor completion, cancellations, and service gaps
CustomersUnderstand booking and repeat-ride behaviour
Problem Statement

Scattered reports slow decisions

  • Revenue performance is difficult to track across time and locations.
  • Cancellation patterns and revenue leakage are not immediately visible.
  • Peak-demand planning depends on manual analysis.
  • Operational teams lack a single executive-friendly dashboard.
Objective

One dashboard. Clear decisions.

Build an interactive Power BI solution that converts raw ride data into actionable insights for operations, revenue, and customer-experience teams.

Insights

Business value delivered

  • Peak-hour demand creates an opportunity for smarter driver allocation.
  • Cancellations represent measurable revenue leakage and need route-level monitoring.
  • Revenue concentration reveals the locations and time windows that matter most.
  • Customer behaviour supports targeted promotions and retention campaigns.
Recommendations

From insight to action

  • Increase driver availability during high-demand hours.
  • Monitor cancellation-prone routes and time slots.
  • Target high-value customer segments with relevant offers.
  • Improve service quality in low-completion locations.
  • Use revenue trends to guide pricing and capacity strategy.
