## Slide 1 — Title

# OLA Ride Operations Analytics
### Power BI Dashboard for Mobility Business Intelligence

**Turning ride-booking data into revenue, operations, and customer insights.**

---

## Slide 2 — Business Context

Ride-sharing platforms generate continuous data across bookings, rides, customers, drivers, locations, and payments.

Business teams need a single view to understand:

- Demand patterns
- Revenue performance
- Cancellations
- Customer behaviour
- Operational efficiency

---

## Slide 3 — Problem Statement

Operational and revenue data is often scattered across multiple reports.

This makes it difficult to identify:

- Where revenue is being generated
- Why rides are cancelled
- When demand peaks
- Which locations need operational attention
- How customer behaviour changes over time

---

## Slide 4 — Project Objective

Build an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps business users monitor ride operations and make faster, data-driven decisions.

**Goal:** Convert raw ride data into clear, actionable business intelligence.

---

## Slide 5 — Solution Overview

The solution includes:

- A multi-page Power BI dashboard
- KPI cards for executive monitoring
- Trend analysis for rides and revenue
- Cancellation and operational analysis
- Customer behaviour insights
- Interactive filters and drill-down capability

---

## Slide 6 — Key KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Rides | Measure overall booking volume |
| Total Revenue | Track business performance |
| Average Fare | Understand customer spend |
| Cancellation Rate | Identify service-quality risk |
| Completion Rate | Measure operational reliability |
| Peak Demand Hours | Support capacity planning |

---

## Slide 9 — Key Insights

- Peak-hour demand requires improved driver allocation.
- Cancellations create measurable revenue leakage.
- Revenue is concentrated in selected locations and time windows.
- Customer behaviour can guide promotions and retention campaigns.
- Operational monitoring helps improve ride completion rates.

---

## Slide 10 — Business Recommendations

- Increase driver availability during high-demand hours.
- Monitor cancellation-prone routes and time slots.
- Use targeted offers for high-value customer segments.
- Improve service quality in locations with low completion rates.
- Review pricing and capacity strategies using revenue trends.
